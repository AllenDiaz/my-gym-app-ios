# ⚡ Tips to Maximize Results

1. **Warm-up 5–8 minutes** plus light sets before heavy lifts.
2. **Progressive overload:** Increase weight or reps weekly.
3. **Rest 60–120 seconds** between sets. Compound lifts may require longer rest.
4. **Protein:** Aim for 1.6–2.2 g per kg of body weight daily.
5. **Sleep:** Target 7–9 hours per night for optimal recovery and growth.
